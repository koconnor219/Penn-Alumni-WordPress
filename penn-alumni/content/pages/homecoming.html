Welcome Back

A celebration of Penn tradition, arts, and community

Penn Alumni and our partners across the University welcome everyone back to campus for three days of exciting programming during Homecoming Weekend. Homecoming Weekend features Arts and Culture at Penn, celebrated both virtually and in-person — a weekend that brings together classmates, friends, and the next generation of Quakers.

Share your favorite memories using #HomeSweetPenn and follow @PennAlumni on Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter for real-time updates.

What to Expect

Three days of Arts, Culture & Tradition

01 — Arts

Gallery Hop & Alumni Arts Fair

Join the Gallery Hop and experience a guided tour showcasing Penn's numerous world-class art galleries. Support fellow alums by browsing handmade pottery, paintings, jewelry, and more at the Alumni Arts Fair.

02 — Culture

Campus Tours & Faculty Talks

Explore campus from an artistic perspective during architecture and sculpture tours. Attend lectures with Penn alumni and faculty, then celebrate Penn's rich culture and connect with the community at gatherings with Penn Traditions, Young Alumni, and Penn Spectrum Programs.

03 — Tradition

Awards Gala & Quaker Fest

Celebrate at the 90th annual Alumni Award of Merit Gala. Raise a toast to dear old Penn at Quaker Fest, then head to Franklin Field to cheer on the Quakers. Dance along to student and alumni bands at the 12th annual Blutt Band Slam.

Travel

Getting to Campus

Philadelphia International Airport (PHL) is 25 minutes by car or a quick SEPTA ride to 30th Street Station. From there, campus is a 10-minute walk. Amtrak service connects directly from NYC, DC, and Boston.
