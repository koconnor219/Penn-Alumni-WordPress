About Penn Alumni

We connect, engage, and serve more than 350,000 living Penn alumni across the world — through programs, publications, volunteers, and the people who make it all happen.

Our Mission

Lifelong connection to Penn — and to each other

Penn Alumni Relations exists to nurture lifelong relationships between alumni and the University of Pennsylvania. We organize the events, run the volunteer programs, publish the magazines, manage the directories, and support the staff and councils that keep more than 350,000 alumni connected.

Whatever brings you back — a class reunion, a regional happy hour, a mentoring relationship, a benefit you didn't know you had, or just curiosity about what Penn is doing now — this office is the front door.

Our Story

From 1740 to today

Penn's alumni community is as old as the University itself. From Benjamin Franklin's founding generation to the half-million alumni network of today, generations of Quakers have made Penn what it is — and Penn Alumni has been the connective tissue across them.

What began as a society of returning graduates has grown into a global organization spanning 180+ regional clubs, dozens of affinity groups, professional networks, and class structures reaching back over a century.

  • 1740Benjamin Franklin's founding gift establishes the Academy and Charitable School of Philadelphia, which becomes the University of Pennsylvania.
  • 1875The first General Alumni Society of Pennsylvania is formally organized to coordinate class activities and reunions.
  • 1948The Pennsylvania Gazette is reorganized as the official magazine of Penn alumni — a tradition that continues quarterly today.
  • 1982The Black Alumni Society is founded — the first of dozens of identity-based affinity groups that connect alumni today.
  • 2025Penn Alumni Relations supports 350,000+ alumni worldwide through events, publications, mentoring, and volunteer leadership.

Alumni Benefits

What Penn alumni get

The lifetime perks, services, and access that come with being part of the Penn family — from libraries to career services to insurance discounts.

All Benefits →

MyPenn Profile

Your alumni directory account — find classmates, update your info, and access alumni-only tools. Free for all Penn alumni.

Library Access

Lifetime borrowing privileges at Van Pelt and other Penn libraries, plus alumni access to JSTOR and select digital resources.

Career Services

Career coaching, job board access, and Penn Career Network mentoring — available throughout your career, not just right after graduation.

Quaker Gmail

A free @alumni.upenn.edu Google Workspace account — sign up and see FAQs.

Insurance & Discounts

Group rates on life, auto, home, and travel insurance, plus discounts at hotels, rental cars, and select retailers.

Campus Facilities

Pottruck Health and Fitness Center day passes, museum admission, and access to Penn Athletics events at alumni rates.

Alumni Travel

Curated trips with Penn faculty leading the experience — from cultural immersion in Japan to safari programs in East Africa.

Audit Penn Courses

Sit in on Penn classes (with instructor permission) — a benefit available to alumni at no cost.

Land Acknowledgement

Land Acknowledgement for the University of Pennsylvania

We recognize and acknowledge that the University of Pennsylvania stands on the Indigenous territory known as “Lenapehoking,” the traditional homelands of the Lenape, also called Lenni-Lenape or Delaware Indians. These are the people who, during the 1680s, negotiated with William Penn to facilitate the founding of the colony of Pennsylvania. Their descendants today include the Delaware Tribe and Delaware Nation of Oklahoma; the Nanticoke Lenni-Lenape, Ramapough Lenape, and Powhatan Renape of New Jersey; and the Munsee Delaware of Ontario.
