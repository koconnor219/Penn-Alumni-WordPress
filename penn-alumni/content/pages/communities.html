Once a Quaker, always a Quaker

More than 300,000 Penn alumni live and work in every corner of the world, and they stay connected through the communities that matter most to them. Reconnect with the classmates you walked Locust Walk with, join 124 regional clubs from Philadelphia to Tokyo, or find kinship in an affinity group built around shared identity, background and interests.

Every community hosts events, welcomes new members and carries Penn traditions forward. All you have to do is show up.
