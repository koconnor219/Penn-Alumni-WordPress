Quick Answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I update my address or contact information?

Log in to MyPenn to update your address, email, phone and employment details. You can also send us a note using the form above and choose “Update my contact information.”

How can I find a classmate?

Search the online alumni directory in MyPenn, or visit the Class Directory to reach your class officers, who can help you reconnect.

What is the refund policy for events?

Refund policies vary by event and are listed on each registration page. For most events, cancellations made before the registration deadline receive a full refund. Contact the Events team with questions.
